# OT World: life, airport and mountain balloon

Build `705.26.3-vida-aeroporto`. Save schema remains 705. Tests use isolated local saves, blocked external accounts and native game engines.

The civil airport has a terminal, four helicopters in total (original, tour, OTTOVIAS and rescue), and original, tour and cargo planes. Each craft has its own boarding interaction and saved parking. The mountain balloon uses the same safe landing, exit, family and parking lifecycle.

Home actions walk to furniture and animate the selected avatar. Effects apply on completion, with cancellation guards. Restaurant orders start visible meals, drinks or handovers. Residents walk, talk, eat, serve, rest and swim. Nearby routines update smoothly; distant routines keep progressing at a lower frequency.

The geographic atlas uses actual positions and roads. Chapter GPS targets advance when the real step finishes. Recorded engine and firearm samples have bundled credits and licenses; they are designed mixes rather than factory recordings of each named vehicle. WAR adds articulated infantry, a new rifle, physical cover, enemy movement and hit feedback while preserving hand/body calibration and tracking-loss gates.

## Validation

244 unit tests passed. The native game smoke passed seven scenarios across five games, including three kart screen sizes and a real three-lap race. The family flow passed Thiago/Thamis selection, all/solo/subset parties, persistence, pets and the original family world. Home, airport and city browser suites passed. Package/public proof is recorded separately after completion.

In `coherence.json`, under the same Chromium software renderer, beach draw calls fell from 3,417 to 2,236 and city calls from 770 to 636. Ninety simulation steps fell from approximately 3.9–4.7 seconds to 226–264 ms in that run. The parked emergency fleet had been recalculating world routes on every traffic update. Rail batching preserves every instance and shared geometry, and covered house interiors no longer draw across the whole world.

These diagnostics are not Android device FPS. Physical camera accuracy, speakers and live two-phone multiplayer are not certified here. Brazilian vehicle artwork remains below the requested reference fidelity; the approved Fusca and Ferrari assets are preserved.
